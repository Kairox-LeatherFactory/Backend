Executive Summary 
This document describes the full end-to-end BOM Procurement Workflow — a six-stage automated system 
that transforms client order and specification inputs into a production -ready material confirmation. The  
workflow integrates AI-driven document analysis, live inventory reconciliation, and automated supplier 
communication to eliminate manual bottlenecks and accelerate production release timelines.

MD/Direct Manager Submission 
Secure document upload initiating the procurement cycle 
 
The workflow is initiated when the MD/Direct Manager authenticates and logs into the application. Upon 
access, the MD/Direct Manager is presented with a dedicated upload screen designed to accept two 
mandatory documents simultaneously. 
 
Key Inputs & Functions 
 
Order Sheet Contains item quantities, SKUs (Stock Keeping Unit), and delivery dates required for 
fulfillment. 
Specification Sheet Contains physical and technical parameters including dimensions, materials, and 
tolerances. 
Process Steps 
 
 
 
AI Analysis and BOM Generation 
Automated extraction and structured BOM compilation from submitted documents 
 
Once both documents are received, the AI engine begins parallel analysis. It performs cross -document 
reconciliation — reading the Order Sheet for procurement quantities and the Specification Sheet for material 
requirements — then compiles these into a unified, structured Bill of Materials. 
 
Key Inputs & Functions 
 
Data Extraction The AI extracts item codes, descriptions, quantities, material, color, sizes, thickness as 
well as comments from the Order Sheet. 
Specification Mapping Specification requirements are mapped to corresponding material and component 
needs from the Spec Sheet. 
Process Steps 
AI engine reads the Order Sheet and identifies all line items, SKUs (Stock Keeping Unit), quantities, and delivery 
dates.

AI Cross-Document Reconciliation Logic 
Once both documents are ingested, the AI engine performs a structured reconciliation across four dimensions: (1) 
Style / Reference Matching — the customer reference CR1-02F5-PL02 on the Order Sheet is cross-matched 
against style CRI 02F5 PL02 on the BOM sheet to confirm both documents relate to the same garment; (2) 
Quantity Propagation — the total order quantity (60 pcs) is multiplied against each material yardage figure to 
calculate bulk material requirements (e.g. 34.5 dm²/garment x 60 = 2,070 dm² of Sheep Glass required); (3) 
Specification Annotation — freeform notes on the Order Sheet (such as the handwritten instruction regarding 
goat suede panel placement, and the material thickness specification of 0.6–0.7 m/m) are extracted and appended 
as annotations on the relevant BOM line items; (4) Price Capture — unit prices for all materials and cost 
components are captured from the BOM sheet and recorded against each line item in the draft BOM, providing cost 
visibility at the point of MD/DIRECT MANAGER review.

MD Review and Approval 
Human oversight gate — review, edit, and formal BOM approval 
 
The Managing Director receives a notification and opens the AI-generated BOM in the review interface. This 
stage represents the primary human decision point in the workflow, where the MD exercises full editorial 
control over the BOM before committing it for procurement action. 
 
Key Inputs & Functions 
 
Edit Capability The MD can adjust quantities, swap materials, or add and remove line items as 
required. 
Approval Action Once satisfied with the BOM content, the MD formally approves the document. 
Process Steps 
 
 
 
 
 
 
STAGE 3

response to the mail that the draft BOM is awaiting review. 
 
2 MD opens the BOM in the review interface and inspects all line items. 
 
3 Adjustments are made as required — modifying quantities, specifications, or line items. 
 
4 MD approves the BOM via the formal approval action. 
 
5 Approval locks the document, preventing further edits, and automatically triggers the inventory check. 
Approval Lock 
Upon MD approval, the BOM is cryptographically locked. No further modifications can be made to the 
approved version. A full revision history is maintained, including all edits made during the review period, 
the approving user identity, and the timestamp of approval.

Inventory Check 
Real-time stock reconciliation against approved BOM requirements 
 
With the BOM approved and locked, the system automatically initiates a live inventory check. Each line item 
in the approved BOM is cross -referenced against current warehouse stock levels. The system calculates 
the exact shortfall for any item that cannot be fulfilled from existing stock. 
 
Key Inputs & Functions 
 
Stock Comparison Required quantity per BOM line is compared against quantity currently on hand in the 
warehouse system. 
Status Assignment Each line is assigned a status — SUFFICIENT, PARTIAL, or OUT OF STOCK — 
based on availability. 
Process Steps 
 
 
 
 PO-Based Supplier Communication with Escalation 
Email-driven supplier communication with fallback automated calling 
 
For every item identified as having a shortfall, the system generates a Purchase Order (PO) form and sends 
it to the designated supplier via email instead of initiating an immediate automated call. If the supplier does 
not respond within 5 hours of the email being sent, the system triggers an automated outbound call to follow 
up and obtain confirmation. 
 
Key Inputs & Functions 
 
Supplier Lookup The system identifies the designated supplier for each shortfall item based on the 
article code. 
STAGE 4 
STAGE 5 
1 Approved BOM is passed to the inventory management module. 
 
2 For every line item, the system queries live stock levels. 
 
3 A status is assigned : SUFFICIENT (full quantity available), PARTIAL (some stock exists but shortfall present), or 
OUT OF STOCK (zero stock available). 
 
4 For SUFFICIENT, PARTIAL, BALANCE & OUT OF STOCK items, the exact quantity is calculated. 
 
5 All shortfall items are compiled into a procurement list and passed to Stage 5. 
Status Definitions 
SUFFICIENT — Full required quantity is available in stock; no procurement action needed. PARTIAL 
— Some stock exists but is insufficient; the shortfall quantity is forwarded for procurement. OUT OF 
STOCK — Zero units available; the full required quantity is forwarded for procurement.

Completion and Production Release 
Final material-readiness confirmation and queue handoff 
 
Once every line item in the approved BOM has been either fulfilled from existing warehouse stock or 
confirmed by a supplier through the automated response, the system evaluates overall material readiness 
and executes the final stage actions to release the job to production. 
 
Key Inputs & Functions 
 
Readiness Assessment The system verifies that 100% of BOM line items are covered — either by stock on 
hand or confirmed supplier delivery. 
Production Release The job is marked as material-ready, an MD alert is triggered, and the job enters the 
production scheduling queue. 
Process Steps 
System verifies all BOM line items have either SUFFICIENT stock or confirmed supplier quantities. 
 
Overall material readiness status is set to READY. 
 
The MD receives an automated alert confirming material readiness. 
 
The job is formally released to the production scheduling queue. 
 
All procurement records, supplier confirmations, and inventory data are archived against the job record.

Production Queue Entry 
The production scheduling queue entry includes: the approved BOM reference, confirmed material 
availability dates, supplier delivery schedules, and the original client delivery dates from the Order 
Sheet. This allows production planning to schedule manufacturing operations with full material visibility.
